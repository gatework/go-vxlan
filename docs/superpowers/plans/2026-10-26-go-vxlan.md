> **For agentic workers:** Use subagent-driven-development or executing-plans; execute continuously under the user's approval.

**Goal:** 实现设计中的全部 Ruby VXLAN 功能并建立差分验证。
**Architecture:** 根包承载证据和解析；领域服务消费配置事实；CLI 组合连接、清单和文件报告。
